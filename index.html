Privacy Policy for Pixel2PDF

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Pixel2PDF ("we", "our", or "the App") is a PDF utility application designed to help users scan images, select photos, and convert content into PDF documents.

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how information is handled when you use Pixel2PDF.

1. Files, Photos and Documents

Pixel2PDF may request access to photos, images, files, or documents that you choose in order to provide PDF creation and conversion features.

Files selected by you are used only to perform the requested functionality, such as creating or managing a PDF document.

Pixel2PDF does not claim ownership of your photos, documents, or generated PDF files.

2. Camera Permission

If you use a scanning or camera feature, Pixel2PDF may request access to your device camera.

Camera access is used only when you choose to capture an image or document for PDF creation.

3. Device Storage

The App may save generated PDF files to your device or allow you to choose where those files are stored.

Access to device storage is used only where necessary to open, create, save, share, or manage files requested by you.

4. Personal Information

Pixel2PDF does not require users to create an account to use its basic PDF conversion features.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. In-App Purchases

Pixel2PDF may offer optional purchases or premium features through Google Play.

Payments are processed by Google Play. Pixel2PDF does not directly receive or store your complete payment card or banking information.

6. Third-Party Services

The App may use services provided by Google or other service providers where required for functionality such as app distribution, in-app purchases, diagnostics, security, or other app features.

These third-party services may process certain device or technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Google's Privacy Policy can be found at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent user data to third parties.

Information may only be processed or shared when required to provide app functionality, comply with applicable law, protect users, or operate services provided by authorized third-party providers.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through Pixel2PDF.

However, no electronic storage, device, or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Pixel2PDF is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

If we become aware that personal information from a child has been collected contrary to applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Pixel2PDF features, legal requirements, or data practices change.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
